# PISDR — Predictive Intelligence for Supply Chain Disruption and Recovery

**A Leakage-Safe, Uncertainty-Aware Decision Evaluation System for Delivery Disruption Risk**

PISDR investigates one question: *can a predictive decision system identify emerging
supply-chain disruptions, estimate their downstream operational consequences, quantify
prediction uncertainty, and recommend recovery actions under changing conditions?*

The project's contribution is not another disruption classifier. It is a reproducible
evaluation of whether uncertainty-aware predictions actually improve operational
decisions — measured as decision regret, service level, and simulated recovery cost —
under temporal distribution shift, using leakage-safe validation.

## Research questions
- RQ1: How much do random-split evaluations overstate disruption-model performance vs. temporal validation?
- RQ2: Do calibrated uncertainty estimates improve recovery decisions vs. point predictions?
- RQ3: Does downstream impact simulation surface high-impact cases that risk classification misses?
- RQ4: How do recovery policies degrade when forecasts are inaccurate or conditions shift?
- RQ5: Can a multi-objective policy (cost, service risk, uncertainty) beat simple rule-based policies?

Full hypotheses and methodology: `docs/01-research-foundation.md`.

## Data
Real public data (DataCo Smart Supply Chain, NY Fed GSCPI, GDELT, NOAA Storm Events)
kept strictly separate from clearly labeled synthetic data generated by the impact
simulator. No fabricated observations. Dataset documentation: `docs/` (Stage 1).
